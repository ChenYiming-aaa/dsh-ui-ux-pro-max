风格描述

保留草稿感：手绘边框、纸张纹理、胶带与马克笔标注，甚至故意的不对齐。真实、可亲，但不适合需要精确感的工具。

关键词

Hand-drawncollagescanned texturesunfinishedimperfectauthentichumansketchraw markscreative process

别名

Anti-Polish Raw

效果与动效

No smooth transitions, hand-drawn animations, paper texture overlays, jitter effects, sketch reveal

--pencil-color#4A4A4A

--texture-opacity0.3

配色方案

主色 / Primary

Paper White #FAFAF8, Pencil Grey #4A4A4A, Marker Black #1A1A1A, Kraft Brown #C4A77D

辅色 / Secondary

Watercolor washes, pencil shading, ink splatters, tape textures, aged paper tones

强调色在页面底色上的对比度约 6.3:1，可用于正文与图标。
