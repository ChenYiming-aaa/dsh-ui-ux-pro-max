风格描述

单列 680px 内容宽度、18px 正文、几乎无装饰，只保留一个 CTA。阅读体验最好，适合一句话就能说清的产品。

关键词

Minimal textwhite space heavysingle column layoutdirect messagingclean typographyvisual-centricfast-loading

效果与动效

Very subtle hover effects, minimal animations, fast page load (no heavy animations), smooth scroll

--spacing-large4rem
--font-size-body18px

--color-text#1a1a1a
--color-bg#ffffff

配色方案

色值取自风格数据，不足时补充主题令牌

主色 / Primary

Monochromatic primary, white background, single accent color for CTA, black/dark grey text

辅色 / Secondary

Minimal secondary colors, reserved for critical CTAs only, neutral supporting elements

强调色在页面底色上的对比度约 17.4:1，可用于正文与图标。

适用场景

适合 / Best For

  • Simple service landing pages
  • indie products
  • consulting services
  • micro SaaS
  • freelancer portfolios

避开 / Do Not Use For

  • Feature-heavy products
  • complex explanations
  • multi-product showcases
