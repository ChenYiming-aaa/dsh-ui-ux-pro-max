配色方案

色值取自风格数据，不足时补充主题令牌

主色 / Primary

Primary brand, bright secondary colors for feature cards, contrasting accent for CTAs

辅色 / Secondary

Supporting colors for: benefits (green), problems (red/orange), features (blue/purple), social proof (neutral)

强调色在页面底色上的对比度约 6.0:1，可用于正文与图标。

适用场景

适合 / Best For

  • Enterprise SaaS
  • software tools landing pages
  • platform services
  • complex product explanations
  • B2B products

避开 / Do Not Use For

  • Simple product pages
  • early-stage startups with few features
  • entertainment landing pages
