风格描述

用真实光照、多层阴影与景深制造物理存在感，常配 WebGL 或高质量渲染图。视觉说服力强，但资源开销大，需要降级方案。

关键词

Depthrealistic textures3D modelsspatial navigationtactileskeuomorphic elementsrich detailimmersive

效果与动效

WebGL/Three.js 3D, realistic shadows (layers), physics lighting, parallax (3-5 layers), smooth 3D (300-400ms)

设计系统变量

--perspective1000px
--parallax-layers5
--lighting-intensityrealistic
--shadow-depth20-40%
--animation-duration300-400ms

配色方案

色值取自风格数据，不足时补充主题令牌

主色 / Primary

Deep Navy #001F3F, Forest Green #228B22, Burgundy #800020, Gold #FFD700, Silver #C0C0C0

辅色 / Secondary

Complex gradients (5-10 stops), realistic lighting, shadow variations (20-40% darker)

强调色在页面底色上的对比度约 12.5:1，可用于正文与图标。

适用场景

适合 / Best For

  • Gaming
  • product showcase
  • immersive experiences
  • high-end e-commerce
  • architectural viz
  • VR/AR

避开 / Do Not Use For

  • Low-end mobile
  • performance-limited
  • critical accessibility
  • data tables/forms

框架兼容

threejsreact-three-fibercustom

浅色模式有条件支持
深色模式有条件支持

转化导向中Medium
渲染成本高animation / large-images

实现要点

落地清单

  • WebGL/Three.js integrated,
  • 3D models loaded,
  • Parallax 3-5 layers,
  • Realistic lighting verified,
  • Complex shadows rendered,
  • Physics animation smooth 300-400ms

CSS / 技术关键词

transform: translate3d, perspective: 1000px, WebGL canvas, Three.js/Babylon.js library, box-shadow: complex multi-layer, background: complex gradients, filter: drop-shadow()

AI 提示词

Build an immersive 3D interface using realistic textures, 3D models (Three.js/Babylon.js), complex shadows, realistic lighting, parallax scrolling (3-5 layers), physics-based motion. Include skeuomorphic elements with tactile detail.
