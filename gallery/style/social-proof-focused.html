风格描述

把用户评价、客户 Logo、评分与数字指标放在首屏附近，用他人的选择降低决策风险。需要真实素材，否则反噬信任。

关键词

Testimonials prominentclient logos displayedcase studies sectionsreviews/ratingsuser avatarssuccess metricscredibility markers

效果与动效

Testimonial carousel animations, logo grid fade-in, stat counter animations (number count-up), review star ratings

设计系统变量

--avatar-size64px
--logo-height40px
--star-color#FBBF24
--metric-font-size3rem
--testimonial-bg#F9FAFB
--blockquote-border4px solid accent

配色方案

色值取自风格数据，不足时补充主题令牌

主色 / Primary

Primary brand, trust colors (blue), success/growth colors (green), neutral backgrounds

辅色 / Secondary

Testimonial highlight colors, logo grid backgrounds (light grey), badge/achievement colors

强调色在页面底色上的对比度约 4.9:1，可用于正文与图标。

适用场景

适合 / Best For

  • B2B SaaS
  • professional services
  • premium products
  • e-commerce conversion pages
  • established brands

避开 / Do Not Use For

  • Startup MVPs
  • products without users
  • niche/experimental products
