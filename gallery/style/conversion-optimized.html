配色方案

色值取自风格数据，不足时补充主题令牌

主色 / Primary

Primary brand color, high-contrast white/light backgrounds, warning/urgency colors for time-limited offers

辅色 / Secondary

Secondary CTA color (muted), trust element colors (testimonial highlights), accent for key benefits

强调色在页面底色上的对比度约 3.1:1，适合大字与图形，正文需另配深色。

适用场景

适合 / Best For

  • E-commerce product pages
  • free trial signups
  • lead generation
  • SaaS pricing pages
  • limited-time offers

避开 / Do Not Use For

  • Complex feature explanations
  • multi-product showcases
  • technical documentation
