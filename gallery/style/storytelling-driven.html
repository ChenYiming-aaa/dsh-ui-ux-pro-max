风格描述

按章节推进：每屏一个情绪节点，用滚动触发揭示、配图与渐变过渡串联。适合讲一个完整故事，需要强内容策划支撑。

关键词

Narrative flowvisual story progressionsection transitionsconsistent character/brand voiceemotional messagingjourney visualization

效果与动效

Section-to-section animations, scroll-triggered reveals, character/icon animations, morphing transitions, parallax narrative

设计系统变量

--section-min-height100vh
--reveal-duration600ms
--narrative-fontserif
--chapter-spacing8rem
--timeline-coloraccent
--parallax-speed0.5

配色方案

色值取自风格数据，不足时补充主题令牌

主色 / Primary

Brand primary, warm/emotional colors, varied accent colors per story section, high visual variety

辅色 / Secondary

Story section color coding, emotional state colors (calm, excitement, success), transitional gradients

强调色在页面底色上的对比度约 4.9:1，可用于正文与图标。

适用场景

适合 / Best For

  • Brand/startup stories
  • mission-driven products
  • premium/lifestyle brands
  • documentary-style products
  • educational

避开 / Do Not Use For

  • Technical/complex products (unless narrative-driven)
  • traditional enterprise software
